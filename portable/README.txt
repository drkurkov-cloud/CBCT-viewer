CBCT Viewer Portable for Windows x64

1. Extract the entire ZIP archive to a USB drive or local folder.
2. Start "CBCT Viewer.exe".
3. Use the + button to open Import Hub and choose the study source.

Imported DICOM files remain byte-for-byte unchanged and keep their original names.
Portable data is stored beside the application in Data\Studies.

This viewer is a clinical support tool. Verify critical measurements and findings
against the source study and your established diagnostic workflow.
